\subsection{Entry, choice of constants, and exit by bands}\label{meso:constantes}
\begin{lema}[Regular entry at $M$]\label{meso:entrada}
Let the entry tolerances for the observables of Section~\ref{meso:regularidad} and
$0<\alpha_{\rm geo}<1/4$ be given. Suppose $\delta$ is small enough that the deterministic entry errors,
namely the $O(\delta+1/n)$ differences between the macroscopic targets at $M$ and those of that section,
lie strictly within these tolerances, uniformly over the tracked lines. Suppose also that the second
estimate of Lemma~\ref{lema:presupuesto-meso} holds with rate $\lambda$. If the radius $\varrho_{\rm mac}$
and mesh $a_{\rm mac}$ of Proposition~\ref{macro:parada} are sufficiently small and $n$ is sufficiently
large, then on every board of size $M$ whose discrepancy $\Delta$ is smaller than $\varrho_{\rm mac}$, the
observables lie within their entry tolerances simultaneously for all available tracked lines. Moreover,
the probability of reaching $M$ without a prior exit and with $D_{\max}(M)\ge\alpha_{\rm geo} M$ satisfies
$\nu(G_M^-,D_{\max}(M)\ge\alpha_{\rm geo} M)\le C_\lambda e^{-\lambda M}$.
\end{lema}
\begin{proof}
Put $x=M/n$. Uniformly in $f$, $U_f=u_f+O(\delta+1/n)$ and $u_f\ge\kappa_\nu$, so
$W_0U_DU_A=\nu_1(1+O(\delta+1/n))$. For a surviving row or column $\ell$, let $F_\ell(M)$ be its
sum of available weights $W_0$. The macroscopic target on $\ell$ has density $xU_DU_A$ in its
transverse coordinate. Dividing by $x$ and integrating against $W_0$ gives $1+O(\delta+1/n)$,
because the marginal of $\nu_1$ is one. In particular,
\[
 \left|\frac{F_\ell(M)}M-1\right|
 \le \frac{\|W_0\|_\infty}{x}\Delta_F+2\omega_F(a_{\rm mac})+C(\delta+1/n).
\]
Here $\omega_F(a_{\rm mac})$ bounds the oscillation of $W_0$ on each subfiber. Replacing the weight
by one sample value per interval in the sum and the target integral costs at most twice this
oscillation in residual units, since each total mass in macroscopic units is at most $x$.

For a rectangle of type $S\subseteq T$, let $w_S=\prod_{e\in S}W_{0,e}\prod_{e\in T\setminus S}\nu_{1,e}$,
let $Z_S(M)$ be its injective observable, and let $I_*=\int_{[0,1]^4}\prod_{e\in T}\nu_{1,e}$
be the common target. The macroscopic target density for this type is
$x^4\prod_{e\in S}U_{D(e)}U_{A(e)}$. After division by $x^4$ and multiplication by $w_S$, its
integral is $I_*+O(\delta+1/n)$. Write $\omega_S(a_{\rm mac})$ for the maximum oscillation of $w_S$
on a partition box. Then
\begin{align*}
 |Z_S(M)-I_*|
 &\le \frac{\|w_S\|_\infty}{x^4}\Delta_R+2\omega_S(a_{\rm mac})+C(\delta+1/n)\\
 &\quad +\frac{C}{M}+\|w_S\|_\infty\left|\frac{M^4}{(M)_2^2}-1\right| .
\end{align*}
Indeed, the ordinary empirical and target masses in units of $n^4$ are each at most $x^4$ in total.
The $C/M$ term restores injective quadruples with coincident diagonal labels. Each coincidence fixes
one index once the other three are given, so at most $O(M^3)$ such assignments use surviving indices.
The last term changes the normalization from $M^4$ to $(M)_2^2$ and is also $O(1/M)$.

Choose the macroscopic partition to refine the mesoscopic one. For each mesoscopic interval, the
error in the residual row or column proportion is then at most $x^{-1}\Delta_0$. All oscillations
above are $O(a_{\rm mac})$ by Lipschitz regularity, uniformly over the tracked lines and the sixteen
types. Once $\delta$ leaves a strict margin in the entry tolerances, $x\ge\delta$ bounds both
amplification factors. We can choose $\varrho_{\rm mac}$, then a sufficiently fine refining mesh
as in Proposition~\ref{macro:parada}, and finally $n$ large enough for the rounding and
discretization terms. These choices fit all three bounds within the entry tolerances whenever
$\Delta<\varrho_{\rm mac}$.

The bound on $D_{\max}(M)$ is the second estimate of Lemma~\ref{lema:presupuesto-meso} at $M$. The
condition concerns all empty crossings, so we estimate it from the initial board and include unavailable
crossings as well.
\end{proof}
A history has \emph{regular entry} at $M$ if it has no exit up to and including the board of size $M$.
Failure of entry at $M$, whether due to an observable or to the geometry, is a macroscopic exit at the
board of size $M$ (Definition~\ref{def:regular}). This board inherits the macroscopic outer bounds from
its last regular parent. In particular, geometric failure at $M$ has probability at most $C_\lambda
e^{-\lambda M}$, exponentially small in $n$ for the $\delta$ already fixed.

We fix the constants in the following order.
\begin{enumerate}
\item Start with the profile and two classes of the form in~\cite[Section~8.1]{pred}. The \emph{inner
class} has the constants $C_{\rm int},\eta_{\rm int},\gamma_{\rm int}$ of
Proposition~\ref{meso:reconstruccion} and diagonal mass at most $\varepsilon$. The \emph{outer class},
which contains it, has diffusion, vacancy, and gap bounds with strictly weaker constants $C_{\rm
ext},\eta_{\rm ext},\gamma_{\rm ext}$ and diagonal mass at most $\varepsilon_{\rm ext}=2\varepsilon$. We
choose $\varepsilon$ in item~4. The outer bounds allow us to fix the constants for the prescriptions,
increments, and inheritance by each child once and for all.
\item Choose $a_{\rm mes},\varrho_{\rm mes}$ and the tolerances for the fibers, the intervals, and each of
the sixteen terms so that rebalancing and spectral reconstruction hold. Reserve, for example, one quarter
of each individual tolerance for entry. These are the \emph{entry tolerances}.
\item Fix $A\ge4C_{\rm geo}$. With the family of observables fixed, choose $\chi,b_1>0$ so that
$C_H\chi+b_1$ lies strictly within another quarter of every tolerance, absorbing the budget constant.
The first estimate of Lemma~\ref{lema:presupuesto-meso} now fixes $c_\chi>0$, and the martingale
estimates of Proposition~\ref{prop:concentracion-meso} fix positive concentration rates. Choose
$c_{\rm pat}>0$ below all these rates. Their inputs have already been fixed, independently of
$\varepsilon$, $\delta$, and the floor.
\item Now take $\varepsilon>0$ sufficiently small for reconstruction, with $\varepsilon_{\rm
ext}=2\varepsilon$ at most the bound $\varepsilon_0(C_{\rm ext},\eta_{\rm ext},\gamma_{\rm ext})$ of
Theorem~8.1 of~\cite{pred}, and small enough that $2\beta(\varepsilon_{\rm ext})<c_{\rm pat}/4$, where
${\beta(v)=2v/(1-v)}$. Fix $\alpha_{\rm geo}=\varepsilon/(4\|W_0\|_\infty)<1/4$ and choose $\delta>0$ so
that the deterministic entry errors and the term $C_HA\delta$ lie strictly within their tolerances, the
restriction $A(\delta+1/n)+\chi+1/K<1/4$ holds for large $n,K$, and the \emph{second} estimate of
Lemma~\ref{lema:presupuesto-meso}, for the geometric event $D_{\max}(k)\ge\alpha_{\rm geo} k$, holds with
some $\lambda>c_{\rm pat}$.
\item Finally, fix $K_0\ge k_0(C_{\rm ext},\eta_{\rm ext},\gamma_{\rm ext})$ sufficiently large, with
$k_0$ from the same theorem. Fix the radius $\varrho_{\rm mac}$ and mesh $a_{\rm mac}$ in the macroscopic
range according to Lemma~\ref{meso:entrada}. Then take $n$ sufficiently large for this $\delta$. These
choices absorb rounding errors, the $O(1/K_0)$ terms, and the macroscopic remainders. The conditions
defining the inner class, including the diagonal mass bound $\le\varepsilon$, are independent of the floor
$K$.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{proposicion}[Exit by bands]\label{prop:salida-banda-meso}
There exist $C<\infty$ and $c>2\beta(\varepsilon_{\rm ext})$ such that for each $K_0\le K\le M$ and
$j\ge0$ with $2^jK\le M$,
\[
 \nu\big(\text{regular entry at $M$ and first exit at a size }
              k\in[2^jK,2^{j+1}K)\big)
 \le C e^{-c\,2^jK}.
\]
\end{proposicion}
\begin{proof}
For any floor $K'$ between $K_0$ and $M$, apply Proposition~\ref{prop:concentracion-meso} and the second
estimate of Lemma~\ref{lema:presupuesto-meso} with that floor. On a history with regular entry, outside
their exceptional events, at every size $K'\le k\le M$ reached up to the first exit, each observable is
within its reconstruction tolerance and $D_{\max}(k)<\alpha_{\rm geo} k$. If the first exit occurred in this
range, the board just reached would itself satisfy these conditions and
Proposition~\ref{meso:reconstruccion} would place it in the inner class. Thus an exit requires one of the
exceptional events, of probability $\le Ce^{-cK'}$ with $c=c_{\rm pat}/2$. Taking $K'=2^jK$ with the same
class and constants gives the claim. The cost factor in Section~\ref{balance} is at most
$\exp(C_0+2\beta(\varepsilon_{\rm ext})K')$ on this band, so $c>2\beta(\varepsilon_{\rm ext})$ leaves a
summable exponential in $K'=2^jK$. The probability and the diagonal budget are both taken from the initial
board under $\nu$. A step that reaches $K$ and
violates a condition counts as an exit at $K$.
\end{proof}
